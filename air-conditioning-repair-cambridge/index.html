When your air conditioner needs attention

Air Conditioning Repair Cambridge is a service to consider when your system stops cooling, will not start, or starts acting differently than it normally does. A change in airflow, unusual cycling, or a new sound can also be a reason to have the unit checked. Those signs do not point to just one cause, so it helps to describe what you have noticed instead of trying to name the fault.

If the system still runs, note whether the room feels different throughout the day or whether the problem happens each time it starts. If it has stopped, write down when you last remember it working as expected. These small details give the technician a useful place to begin.
