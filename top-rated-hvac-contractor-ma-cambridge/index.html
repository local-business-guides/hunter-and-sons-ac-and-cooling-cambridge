What to share and what to avoid

A straightforward description is more useful than a guess about the cause. Before calling, consider writing down:

  • How long you’ve noticed the issue
  • Whether it happens all the time or only at certain times
  • Any recent change in how the equipment is operating

Avoid assuming that one symptom points to one specific repair. Also, don’t open or adjust equipment if you’re uncertain about what you’re doing.
