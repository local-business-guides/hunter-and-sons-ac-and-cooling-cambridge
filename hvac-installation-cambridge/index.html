A checklist for choosing and preparing

Gathering a few details can make it easier to explain the project and evaluate the proposed work. Keep the conversation focused on how you use the property, not just on a particular piece of equipment.

  • List the rooms or work areas where comfort is a concern.
  • Describe how the space is used and when people are there.
  • Note what you know about the current heating and cooling equipment.
  • Share any preferences or practical limits that could affect the work area.
  • Ask what steps happen before installation and what you should prepare.

Don’t assume a system that works well elsewhere will be right for your property. Avoid choosing equipment before discussing the space and intended use with a professional. Also, don’t overlook questions about access, the work involved, or how the contractor will explain operation after the installation.
